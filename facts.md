# Facts and figures

**Tax year: 2027** (1 March 2026 to 28 February 2027). SARS names a tax year after the year it
ends in.

Every figure below comes from the linked page, on the date shown. Update this file after each
February budget speech; lessons point here instead of repeating numbers. The official pages are
not openly licensed, so this file states the facts in its own words and links to them.

## Two-pot system

Source: [FSCA two-pot FAQ](https://www.fscamymoney.co.za/Pages/two-pot-system-retirement-system-FAQ.aspx),
checked 2026-11-05. Also: [National Treasury FAQ](https://www.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2024/2024%20Two-pot%20System%20Updated%20%20FAQ%20August%202024.pdf).

Since 1 September 2024, pension, provident and retirement annuity funds split money into three
pots:

| Pot | What goes in | When you can take it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vested pot | Everything saved before 1 September 2024, plus growth | Old rules: in cash when you resign, or one-third in cash at retirement |
| Savings pot | One-third of contributions from 1 September 2024, plus seed capital | One withdrawal per tax year, of at least R2 000 |
| Retirement pot | Two-thirds of contributions from 1 September 2024 | Only at retirement, to buy an annuity (a pension) |

- Seed capital: 10% of the vested pot, up to R30 000, was moved into the savings pot.
- Savings-pot withdrawals are taxed at your marginal income tax rate. Fund fees and any tax you
  owe SARS come off first.
- When you move to a new employer's fund, all three pots move with you.

## Withdrawal lump-sum tax table

Source: [Retirement lump sum benefits](https://www.sars.gov.za/tax-rates/income-tax/retirement-lump-sum-benefits/),
checked 2026-11-05. SARS says this table did not change for the 2026 or 2027 tax year.

Applies when you take money out of a retirement fund in cash before retirement, for example the
vested pot when you resign. The table is cumulative: SARS adds every withdrawal you have ever
taken (since March 2009) and taxes the running total.

| Total withdrawals (R) | Tax (R) |
| --- | --- |
| 0 – 27 500 | 0% |
| 27 501 – 726 000 | 18% of the amount above 27 500 |
| 726 001 – 1 089 000 | 125 730 + 27% of the amount above 726 000 |
| 1 089 001 and above | 223 740 + 36% of the amount above 1 089 000 |

## Retirement lump-sum tax table

Source: same page, checked 2026-11-05. Unchanged for the 2026 and 2027 tax years.

Applies to cash taken at retirement, and to severance benefits. It is also cumulative, and it
counts withdrawals taken earlier, so cashing out before retirement shrinks the tax-free amount
you get here.

| Total lump sums (R) | Tax (R) |
| --- | --- |
| 0 – 550 000 | 0% |
| 550 001 – 770 000 | 18% of the amount above 550 000 |
| 770 001 – 1 155 000 | 39 600 + 27% of the amount above 770 000 |
| 1 155 001 and above | 143 550 + 36% of the amount above 1 155 000 |

## Retiring from a fund

Source: [FSCA two-pot FAQ](https://www.fscamymoney.co.za/Pages/two-pot-system-retirement-system-FAQ.aspx),
checked 2026-11-05.

- You may take up to one-third of the vested pot in cash. The rest of the vested pot and the whole
  retirement pot must buy an annuity.
- Small amounts: if two-thirds of the vested pot plus the retirement pot comes to R165 000 or
  less, you may take it all in cash.

## Living annuity drawdown

Source: [10X living annuity FAQ](https://www.10x.co.za/faq/withdraw-living-annuity) and
[ASISA](https://www.asisa.org.za/media-releases/living-annuity-drawdown-rate-average-drops-below-6-for-the-first-time-since-2011/),
checked 2026-11-05. Not yet checked against an official regulator page.

- You choose an income of 2.5% to 17.5% of the capital each year, and can change it once a year.
- The money is yours and can run out; whatever is left goes to your beneficiaries.

## Retirement fund contributions

Source: [Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/),
checked 2026-11-02.

Contributions to pension, provident and retirement annuity funds are deductible up to 27.5% of the
greater of remuneration or taxable income, capped at R430 000 a year.

## Tax-free savings accounts

Source: [Tax free investments](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/tax-free-investments/),
checked 2026-11-02.

- Annual limit: R46 000 (from 1 March 2026), across all your tax-free accounts together.
- Lifetime limit: R500 000.
- Going over either limit triggers a penalty tax on the excess.
- No tax on interest, dividends or growth inside the account. Withdrawing doesn't give you the
  limit back.

## SASSA Older Person's Grant

Source: [gov.za: Older Persons Grant](https://www.gov.za/services/social-benefits-retirement-and-old-age/old-age-pension),
checked 2026-11-05. The page is undated; confirm on sassa.gov.za before relying on it.

| | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly grant, age 60 to 74 | up to R2 400 |
| Monthly grant, age 75 and older | up to R2 420 |
| Income limit, single / married | R86 280 / R172 560 a year |
| Asset limit, single / married | R1 227 600 / R2 455 200 |

You apply in person at a SASSA office. Applying is free, and the grant is backdated to the date
you applied.

## Checking a provider and complaining

Checked 2026-11-05.

- Check that a financial services provider (FSP) is licensed on the
  [FSCA website](https://www.fsca.co.za), by both its name and its FSP number. They must match,
  and the licence must be active. FSCA consumer line: 0800 20 3722
  ([FSCA](https://www.fscamymoney.co.za/Pages/two-pot-system-retirement-system-FAQ.aspx)).
- Complaints about a retirement fund go to the
  [Pension Funds Adjudicator](https://pfa.org.za), free and in writing. Government Employees
  Pension Fund (GEPF) members use the Government Employees Pension Ombud instead.
- Complaints about a financial adviser go to the [FAIS Ombud](https://www.faisombud.co.za), free.
