# Retirement and investing in South Africa

How saving for retirement and investing work in South Africa, taught by your AI assistant: the
two-pot system, changing jobs, tax-free savings, retirement annuities, fees, scams and retiring.

## This is not financial advice

This course explains how things work. It never tells you what to buy or whether to withdraw. For
your own situation, use a financial adviser licensed by the FSCA (lesson 7 shows how to check).
